dewkiln layout
==============

wickmill/boot.rs dispatches clasp.

Pipeline crates:
- hopcue/
- millrib/
- restpit/
- urncue/
- shardfold/
- lidcue/

Day files live in /app/daywell.
clasp writes /app/bloturn/ledger.json and /app/bloturn/check.json.
It packs /app/packbay/stage then unpacks /app/packbay/latest with the same calendar names.
millrib/sheet.tpl at /app/millrib/sheet.tpl fills check.json; its placeholders stay in the template.
Fixture copies of the day files sit at /app/seed/daywell.

check.json object:
- probe (the string live)
- matched (integer live daywell row total at or before the wall)
- taken (integer, same total)

CI floor integer lives in /app/floorcue/MIN.txt.

Extra daywell files still use names like d20260908.tbl and d20260914.tbl.


Rebuild the binary with /app/wick.sh after source edits.
