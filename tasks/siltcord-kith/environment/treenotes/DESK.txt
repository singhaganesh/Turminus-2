Desk JSON keys: timezone, cluster, log_events, member_count, source_kith, walk_sig.

sys.conf uses key=value lines. timezone and cluster come from the sys.conf payload the mill applies. When more than one sys.conf payload exists under names that match after a leading ./ is dropped, timezone and cluster are the keys from the payload that remains after the walk. log_events is the lexicographically sorted unique event strings from every app.log payload. member_count is the integer of regular-file payloads applied. source_kith is the archive basename.
