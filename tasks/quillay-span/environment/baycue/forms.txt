layout.qmap
  First line: QMAP1
  Later lines: path device inode text_vma text_off text_sz (row length 6)
  device and inode are decimal from the mapped file.
  text_vma, text_off, text_sz are decimal integers from the executable load segment.

flame.qprf
  First line: QPRF1
  Later lines: pc_hex name
  pc_hex is lowercase hex without a 0x prefix.
  name is the live symbol covering that PC in /app/hearthbin/span.elf.

pc.lst
  One lowercase hex PC per line.

load.c
  tamp compiles /app/spanhearth/load.c; /app/spanhearth/wave.txt selects the generation.
