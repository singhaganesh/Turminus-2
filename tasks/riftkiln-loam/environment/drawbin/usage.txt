usage: riftkiln sift SRC DEST

DEST is an object with yard (string) and members (array). Each members item has kind peg, pen, or clip. A peg has tag and mark strings. A pen has stem (string) and members (same array shape). A clip has stem (string).

A clip ident that appears after an inner pen close and before that inner pen's enclosing pen closes must be a clip object in the enclosing pen's members array, immediately after that inner pen object. At that cut the enclosing members array has length 2 when the inner pen is the only prior member: the inner pen, then the clip. The inner pen must not grow a clip property.
