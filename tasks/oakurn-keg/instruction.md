The crash desk on this image prints `/app/inkpit/counts.json` through `/app/bin/oakurn board`. Urns sit under `/app/caskbay`. `/app/bin/oakurn pour` loads one urn. `/app/bin/oakurn steep` is the follow-on ops already wired after each pour; it returns 0 today. Desk numbers stall under overlapping pours and only jump once the floor goes quiet. Field names live in `/app/treenote/forms.txt`. Layout lives in `/app/treenote/TREE.txt`. `/app/bin/oakurn` begins with bytes `\x7fELF`.

`counts.json` carries `days` (calendar day string to integer totals) and `covers` (identifier of the urn the mill last folded). Pour then steep leaves `covers` on that urn's identifier and adds the urn's events on the day written inside the urn, not a wall stamp on the mill. Steep also rewrites `/app/inkpit/emit.inc`. Steep exits non-zero when it did no mill work. C++ modules named from `/app/treenote/TREE.txt` need source updates under `/app`. Manual copies of inkpit products are insufficient; steep must regenerate `counts.json` and `emit.inc`. The verifier reruns `/app/bin/oakurn pour`, `/app/bin/oakurn steep`, and `/app/bin/oakurn board`.
