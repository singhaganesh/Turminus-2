Ops left `/app/bin/gasketd` on this image. `bind` reads every card under `/app/buscards` and refreshes `/app/mistwell/roster.json`. `jab` takes one integer and prints `{"wire":N,"reply":S}`. `cork` writes `/app/corkbay/seal.json` with `served` (LABEL values in card order) and `status`. Source fixes under `/app` are required. Modules live in directories listed by `/app/opscribe/MAP.txt`. Static copies of the roster or seal objects are not enough. After that work `/app/bin/gasketd` must start with `\x7fELF`. The verifier reruns `/app/bin/gasketd bind`, `/app/bin/gasketd jab`, and `/app/bin/gasketd cork`.

A newly added card still lets `bind` and `cork` finish, yet `jab` of its WIRE prints `fallthrough` instead of that card's REPLY. Cards already under `/app/buscards` at the start must each answer `jab` with their own REPLY. An integer that no card lists must print `fallthrough`. After `bind` rewrites the roster, `jab` of a WIRE that only exists on a card added later must still print `fallthrough`, and `cork` must then exit non-zero with `status` not `sealed`. Each card has a `LABEL` line, a `WIRE` line (integer), and a `REPLY` line.
