gasket hopper layout
====================

gaskcli/boot.rs dispatches bind, jab, and cork.

Pipeline modules:
- loomkit/  roster writer
- rimcap/   compiled span
- hopmat/   WIRE replies used by jab
- lidpit/   seal writer
- slipread/ card parse

Cards live in /app/buscards.
bind refreshes /app/mistwell/roster.json and /app/loomkit/emit.lst.
jab still answers from the hopper already inside /app/bin/gasketd.
A new roster line from bind does not enroll that WIRE into jab.
cork writes /app/corkbay/seal.json.
If jab of any card WIRE still prints fallthrough after bind, cork must fail
and status must not be sealed.
Fixture copies of the cards sit at /app/cardbak/buscards.

Rebuild the binary with /app/stoke.sh after source edits.
