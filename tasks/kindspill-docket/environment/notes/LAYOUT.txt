kindspill mill layout
=====================

cratebin/main.rs dispatches brew and peek.

Pipeline modules:
- kindmill/  name list writer
- emberkit/  rank table writer
- filewell/  docket writer

Sheets live in /app/sanitdesc.
Brew refreshes /app/spillbay/kinds.ord, /app/rankbin/ranks.bin, /app/docket/filed.bin.
Fixture copies of the sheets and cache sit at /app/seed/sanitdesc and /app/seed/warm.cache.
Brew reads /app/endpin/KEEP_TAIL.txt as an ops sidecar.
`/app/coldstore/prior.bin` is an archived docket from an older mill; brew must not treat it as the live file.

Rebuild the binary with `/app/hull.sh` after source edits.
