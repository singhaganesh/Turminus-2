A whole card has four lines:

FIN1
thread=<name>
op=<verb>
END

Each line ends with a newline. The name and verb on the card are the
same tokens passed on the command line. A whole card is those four
lines and nothing more. After an interruption, the dest file is either
that whole card or absent — never a partial file.

Names that appear on this mill include desk-k4, keel-hold-r9, n1, and
rib-yard-m3. Verbs include splice-harbor-overlong-token-alpha-zz,
quay-splice-harbor-overlong-token-beta-yy, fold_zeta_ww, and v1.
