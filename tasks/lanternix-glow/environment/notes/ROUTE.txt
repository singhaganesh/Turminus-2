Pipeline modules live under /app/oxbind, /app/packbay, and /app/milldesk.
Cast refreshes generated units under /app/packbay/units and rewrites /app/packbay/reg.go.
Readout: /app/bin/lanternix readout --lamp FILE writes /app/glowbank/readout.json.
Cast: /app/bin/lanternix cast
/app/bin/lanternix is the ELF mill from milldesk. milldesk/Makefile has a ship target.
Slot grammar for cards and lamps is in /app/notes/GRAMMAR.txt.
Readout must honor the lamp FILE argument; sample files under /app/lamps are not the only recipe lamps.
