peatwick mill
=============

bin/peatwick dispatches kindle. Mill sources under /app/gaitmod, /app/joincue,
and /app/stillbay must agree with the mill that CLI actually runs. Packed copies
the CLI loads live under /app/bin/peatwick.d, including
/app/bin/peatwick.d/gaitmod/stride.rb.

Pipeline modules:
- gaitmod/  change-set walk
- joincue/  coverage join
- stillbay/ empty-pick gate

Desk files live in /app/livefold.
Snapshot directories live in /app/snapurn/seals.
kindle refreshes /app/inkvat/sieve.bin and /app/inkvat/pick.json.
Coverage cards live in /app/lutcards.
Ops sidecar: /app/opside/SEAL_FIRST.txt.

Mill sources use FOLD SNAP SCRATCH ROSTER LEDGER as path constants for
/app/livefold, /app/snapurn/seals, /app/inkvat/delta.lst,
/app/inkvat/pick.json, and /app/inkvat/sieve.bin.
