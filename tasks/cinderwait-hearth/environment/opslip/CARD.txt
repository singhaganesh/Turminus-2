watch.json on a successful watch:
  seen — integer gather word from the packed mill
  bag — basename of the hopbag (alpha.bag), not the full command-line path
  note — kiln stamp taken from MARK.txt

The mill stores the wait-board integer the gatherer publishes onto the packed mill. That integer is not the first-line token of the hopbag. A never-publish bag (first-line integer 0) is not a published bag; watch must not write a success ledger for it.

Grade-time bags may sit outside /app/hopbags. One of those bags is also named on this card as /app/hopbags/elm.bag.
