# Lane cards

Search order is `A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`.

| Lane | Height lid (cm) | Span (cm) | Mass lid (kg) |
| A1 | 180 | 2000 | 8000 |
| A2 | 180 | 2000 | 8000 |
| B1 | 420 | 2000 | 18000 |
| B2 | 420 | 2000 | 18000 |

Deck `A` also has a paired kilogram lid of 11000 that both `A1` and `A2` share.
Deck `B` has a paired kilogram lid of 28000 that both `B1` and `B2` share.
Sister lanes on a deck must stay within 3000 kg of each other once both sisters
already hold a plate. Opening the first plate on a deck is free. Opening the
second sister lane is free.

Kilograms already packed in a lane count against that lane's mass lid. Kilograms
already packed on the same deck letter count against the paired lid. A plate
that fits leftover centimetres but not leftover lane kilograms, or that would
break the paired lid or sister trim, must try the next lane. Leftover is
computed after earlier boarding seats, not after the order the names appear in
the roll.

Decks: `A` lid 180 cm; `B` lid 420 cm. Low plates that still fit on `A` must
not occupy `B`.
