Station geometry
================

Frame size: 160 x 120
Rate: 12 fps
Horizontal scan row: y = 70 (top of frame is y = 0)

Drive:

    python3 /app/varnish/mill.pyc CLIP

CLIP is an mp4 path. Success writes /app/inkwell/sheet.toml:

    crossings = <integer>

Worked sample: /app/shown.mp4 -> crossings = 3

Similar silhouettes that share the scan still count as separate when each
centroid completes top-to-bottom, including overlap, one body shading another,
or a merge into one region that later splits. A long smear of shade that
sweeps the scan is not a body.

Unreadable CLIP: non-zero exit, do not write /app/inkwell/sheet.toml.
Example unreadable path:

    python3 /app/varnish/mill.pyc /tmp/no-such-fordwick.mp4

Fold blot, tether, spout, and the spanwell driver using the kiln notes.
