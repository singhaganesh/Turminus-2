`/app/bin/kilncli assay` opens `/app/vats/worker.vat` and exits with unknown-column `attempt_slot`. `/app/vats/bench.vat` already lists that column. `/app/bin/kilncli spill` writes `/app/vats/worker.vat`. `/app/vats/plaster.vat` and the worker list `attempt_slot` as a `cols:` token and share a final `applied:` stem equal to the lexicographic latest `*.sql` stem in `/app/inkstack/migrations/`. Assay prints `schema_version=<id>` with that stem. `/app/vats/assay.ok` is written only then. A worker whose applied stem lags that head makes assay exit non-zero; `assay.ok` is false. Spill of a source whose applied stem lags that head, or a newer `*.sql` than that source stem, exits non-zero. Vat files are `KILNVAT 1` with `applied:`, `table:`, `cols:`, and `row:` lines.

Kiln modules named in `/app/baydocs/BAY_NOTES.txt` live under `/app`. Source updates under `/app` are required. Hand-written vat files are not sufficient. The verifier reruns `/app/bin/kilncli assay` without rebuilding.
