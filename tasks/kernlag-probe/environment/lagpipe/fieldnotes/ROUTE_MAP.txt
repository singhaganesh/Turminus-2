lagpipe synctab wiring notes (internal)

Modules linked by `make -C /app/lagpipe bind` participate in synctab:
  layout pick — chooses sched_entity table input for synctab
  types writer — materializes synctab_out/layout.tbl from the picked table
  tag policy gate — decides whether procance lines are acceptable on this image
  probe emitter — decodes memvault captures through layout.tbl offsets

Field layout rules and CLI contract: /app/lagpipe/fieldnotes/FIELD_RULES.txt
