Difficulty Explanation
The mill reports success while desk totals freeze under overlapping ingest. The aggregate stays internally consistent, so a green mill looks finished. Coverage identity, calendar bucketing, and idle mill status live in separate modules; fixing one leaves the others wrong. Frontier models, including Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6, can treat the green mill as done, paste the desk file, or take an exclusive-hold helper that still skips real mill work.

Solution Explanation
Work started from the stalling desk and a mill that always returned success. Tracing showed an in-flight marker skipping mill work, a leftover coverage stamp, and totals parked on a mill wall day instead of each record's own day. The fix proceeds despite the marker, stamps the record just ingested, and folds by the record day field. The linked mill is rebuilt so those source changes actually run.

Verification Explanation
Checks ingest a record, run the mill, and read the desk object plus mill stdout. They require the coverage identifier of the ingested record, day totals on the record's own calendar day, a non-zero mill when no work happened, and recovery after the desk file is emptied. A native binary must remain the mill. Pasted desk files and script replacements of the mill do not satisfy the checks.
