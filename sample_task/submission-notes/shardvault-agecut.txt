## Difficulty Explanation

Same-host write-and-cut sessions already look healthy, so agents that only smoke the happy path miss the hour-scale early-or-late retention skew. The break shows up when creation and evaluation run under unequal civil zones, and the bias flips around daylight-saving transitions, which looks like ops configuration rather than stamp provenance. Aligning every runner to one shared locale is an attractive shortcut that still leaves stamps ambiguous under harness zone overrides. Correctness needs seal, parse, and age comparison to agree on one absolute instant, not a single-file tweak. Frontier models often stop after making one host green or after editing the farm locale file.

## Solution Explanation

Reproduce put under one locale and agecut under another with the same absolute creation and evaluation instants. Trace how creation text is sealed into the ledger, how that text is cracked back into a time value, and how age is compared to the configured lifetime. Make the sealed text carry an absolute offset, parse it as that instant on every host, and compare ages on absolute timelines rather than civil wall clocks. Rebuild the binary through the documented reforge path and re-run put/agecut instead of editing report files by hand.

## Verification Explanation

Checks rebuild from sources and drive put and agecut under several locales, including a Pacific daylight-saving spring-forward window. They require matching absolute creation stamps across writer locales, identical parse results under reader locales, exact lifetime boundary retain/drop decisions, sorted report fields, and a ledger reduced to kept rows. Editing only the shared-locale config while leaving stamps ambiguous fails. Stuffing precomputed reports without a working regenerated pipeline also fails.
