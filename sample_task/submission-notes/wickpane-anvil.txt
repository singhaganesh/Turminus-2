Solution Explanation

Work started from the operator symptom: regen green, two live takes of one fault on different panes. The grammar in the ops notes was applied independently of the ledger. Three cooperating stages were wrong: module tokens still carried plus tails, fold-marked rows stayed as their own frames, and kernel-helper modules survived because they were compared before the plus cut. The nibble-trim helper was left unused. After those stages were corrected, the mill was rebuilt as a native binary and both regen and live filing were run again so the ledger and the filing map matched the grammar.
